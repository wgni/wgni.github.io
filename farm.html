Farm Overview

A Living Proof of
What's Possible

WGNI Farm in Kobape, Ogun State is not just a farm — it is a model. Every operation on the farm is run with formal SOPs, daily reporting, and digital systems that track everything from feed balance to harvest yield.

The farm serves as both a production facility and a demonstration ground for investors and partners exploring agricultural opportunities in Nigeria.

What We Grow & Raise

Farm Operations

Three core production units, each managed with dedicated SOPs and performance tracking.

Greenhouse Cultivation
High-yield greenhouse vegetable production including bell peppers. Drip irrigation and controlled environment farming for maximum output and consistent quality.
Broiler & Layer Poultry
Structured poultry management across broiler and layer units with formal health protocols, feed management, and performance monitoring systems.
Catfish Management
Active catfish pond operations with regular stocking, feeding schedules, and water quality management for healthy, productive yields across all ponds.
