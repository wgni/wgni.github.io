Our Story

A Company Built to
Last & Scale

WGNI was founded on a simple but powerful conviction — that Nigeria's agricultural and hospitality sectors hold enormous untapped potential, and that what they need most is not just capital, but structured execution and expert guidance.

From our model farm in Kobape, Ogun State to our agriculture consulting arm that has supported landmark projects like the Fashola Cassava Factory, WGNI has built a track record of delivering real results in the field.

Today, WGNI operates across four distinct verticals — agriculture consulting, model farm operations, beach house hospitality, and drenching (sand recovery) contracting — each built to create lasting value.

"We partner with farmers, agribusinesses, and investors to design and drive sustainable growth in agriculture."
Our Mission

To design and drive sustainable agricultural growth and build multi-sector enterprises that transform Nigeria's economy.

Headquartered in Ikoyi, Lagos  ·  Model Farm in Kobape, Ogun State
