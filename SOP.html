General Farm Operations
Governing framework for the day-to-day management of WGNI Farm across all staff, contractors, facilities, and compliance requirements.
All StaffContractorsSecurityProcurement
SEC 1Purpose & Scope▼

This manual establishes the standard operating framework for the day-to-day management of WGNI Farm. It governs facility management, cleanliness, weed control, security, personnel conduct, store management, procurement, and contractor oversight across all farm areas.

Applies to all permanent staff, casual workers, security personnel, and contractors — including weekends and after hours.

Scope: greenhouse facilities, open field, fish pond, poultry area, security house, staff quarters, store, factory facility, and all access roads within the farm perimeter.
SEC 4Organisational Structure & Hierarchy▼

The Farm Manager and Farm Supervisor are parallel positions — neither is superior to the other.

SEC 10Security Management▼
  • At least one security officer must be at the main gate at all times — day, night, weekdays, and weekends.
  • When one officer steps out, another must assume gate duty before departure.
  • Every officer maintains a step-out register: date, time out, destination, reason, time back, signature.
  • All visitors must sign the visitor register before entering — no exceptions regardless of identity.
  • Delivery vehicles must be logged: registration, driver name, purpose, time in/out.

SEC 8–9Cleanliness, Sanitation & Weed Management▼
Every Saturday: whole-farm deep clean mandatory for all residents and farm workers. Farm Manager supervises and confirms completion.
  • Chemical herbicides must never be applied inside greenhouse kits, over fish ponds, or directly over open field crops.
  • Minimum 5 m from any greenhouse kit wall before spraying begins.
  • Minimum 10 m from any fish pond edge.
  • Full PPE required at all times during chemical application.
  • No cooking inside any building. Outdoor cooking only — minimum 5 m from any building or flammable material.

Greenhouse Operations
Bell pepper production across all active greenhouse kits. Access, fertigation, irrigation, crop monitoring, and pest management.
Kit 1 — 500 sqm · 1,200 standsKit 7 — 250 sqm · 600 stands
SEC 1Access & Security Protocols▼

Only three personnel may enter any active greenhouse kit: the assigned Greenhouse Worker, the Agronomist, and the Farm Manager. No other person — including visitors, buyers, or contractors — may enter under any circumstance.

  • Remove or cover external footwear at the entrance. Dedicated greenhouse footwear for each kit must be worn inside at all times.
  • Wash hands with soap and water before touching any plant, bed, irrigation line, or tool.
  • Close and secure the door immediately on entry. It must remain closed at all times while anyone is inside.
  • When moving between kits — wash hands and clean footwear before entering the next kit.
  • Tools and equipment assigned to one kit must not be transferred without cleaning and disinfection using Hypo solution.
SEC 2Environmental & Perimeter Conditions▼
  • Minimum 2-metre clearance zone around every active greenhouse kit at all times.
  • Grass must not exceed 5 cm within the clearance zone.
  • No standing water, organic debris, or stored materials within the 2-metre zone.
  • Gravelled drainage channel at the base of the exterior wall must remain clear at all times.
  • Herbicides must never be used inside an active greenhouse.
Mulch film damage — any tear, lifted section, or widened planting hole must be reported to the Agronomist on the day it is observed.
SEC 3Water Supply & Pumping▼

Water is transferred from the main storage tank to the greenhouse header tank using a 2HP mud pump. From the header tank, water flows through drip irrigation lines during each fertigation run.

  • Check header tank level every morning before irrigation and every evening before the evening run.
  • Pump when header tank drops below half capacity.
  • Never allow the header tank to run completely empty — air enters drip lines causing uneven delivery.
  • A running pump must never be left unattended.

SEC 5Crop Monitoring & Observation▼

A full observation walkthrough must be conducted at every morning and evening session. Walk slowly through every row from end to end.

  • Check every plant for wilting, colour change, drooping, or unusual markings.
  • Inspect drip emitters along each row — all must be dripping. Report blocked emitters before the next run.
  • Check mulch film for tears, lifting, or widened holes.
  • Inspect upper and lower leaf surfaces of any plant showing discolouration.
  • Any plant showing disease symptoms must be flagged to the Agronomist same day. Do not treat without instruction.

SEC BBase Tank Management▼

The Ecoflow hybrid solar pump is the primary method for filling base tanks. It operates automatically from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, switching between solar and generator AC backup as needed.

  • Never allow combined base tank storage to drop below 5,000L.
  • Visually assess all 6 tanks every morning before any pumping or site activity begins.
  • Allow Ecoflow solar to run first. Use electric pump only when solar is insufficient.
  • If base tanks approach 5,000L — pause ALL non-greenhouse draws immediately.
  • Cover all base tank inlets when not in use.

Open Field & Orchard
Seed beds 1–3 and orchard — bed preparation, irrigation, crop monitoring, chemical application, and harvest standards.
Beds 1–3Sweet Corn · TomatoesMango · Orange
SEC 1Access & Security▼
  • Assigned field workers, the Agronomist, and the Farm Manager are authorised to access seed beds during working hours.
  • Visitors may observe from the field perimeter only. They must not enter active beds without Farm Manager authorisation.
  • No vehicle may enter the orchard without Farm Manager authorisation — causes soil compaction and root damage.
  • No livestock may access the orchard area at any time.
  • All tools must be returned to the store at the end of every working day. No tool is left in the field overnight.
SEC 2Bed Preparation Standards▼
StandardSpecification
Bed width90–100 cm across the top
Bed height15–20 cm above ground level
Walkway between bedsMinimum 50 cm
Soil tilling depthAt least 25–30 cm
Drip lineOne per bed, centre. Tested before mulch film applied.
Mulch filmBlack polythene — pulled taut, anchored both sides. Not reused without Agronomist approval.
⚠ Do not lay mulch film over an untested drip line.
SEC 3Irrigation Management▼
  • Typical frequency: once or twice daily during establishment and active growth — in absence of rainfall.
  • Do not irrigate if beds have received adequate natural rainfall. Check soil moisture under mulch film first.
  • Walk the full length of every active bed and confirm all drip lines are intact before every run.
  • Do not leave the field during an active irrigation run.
SEC 6Orchard Management▼
  • Base circle around each tree must be weed-free and mulched at all times.
  • Inter-row grass must remain below 15 cm. Manual cutting preferred near trees.
  • Graft union must always remain above the soil surface — never bury during mulching.
  • Formative pruning in first 3 years is the Agronomist's decision only.
  • Boundary markers must be inspected and confirmed intact during every Saturday inspection.
Any tree showing pest or disease signs must be flagged to the Agronomist on the day of observation. No chemical application without Agronomist authorisation.

Poultry Operations
Broiler and turkey production — biosecurity, daily routines, feed guide, weight monitoring, slaughter protocols, and sanction grid.
Broilers — 8 weeksTurkey — 12 weeks
SEC 1Biosecurity & Access▼
⚠ Only the assigned poultry worker and the Farm Manager may enter the poultry house. No exceptions — including management, visitors, and family members.
  • Padlock must be locked every time the worker steps out, even briefly.
  • A bucket of sodium hypochlorite solution at the entrance of every pen at all times. Both feet must be dipped fully before every entry.
  • Solution: 50–75ml Hypo in 10–15 litres clean water. Replace every 2 days or when visibly dirty.
  • Each pen has dedicated boots. Pen A boots stay at Pen A — they do not cross. External footwear is forbidden inside any pen.
  • Entry sequence (multiple pens): young birds first, then older. Healthy pen first, then suspect or isolation. Never reverse.
SEC 2–4Daily Routines: Morning · Midday · Evening▼

Morning — 7:00 AM

  1. Arrive. Put on pen boots. Dip boots in disinfectant bucket. Wash hands.
  2. Conduct full Morning Observation Walkthrough.
  3. Remove any dead birds immediately with gloves. Report every death to Farm Manager before 8:00 AM.
  4. Empty, rinse, and refill all drinkers with clean fresh water.
  5. Inspect feed before adding — smell it, check texture, look for mould. No feed enters without passing this check.
  6. Give morning feed. Check ventilation. Fill in Form A. Step out. Lock padlock.

Midday Heat Check — 12:00–2:00 PM

Do not feed during the midday check. Feeding during peak heat worsens heat stress. If birds are panting with wings spread — report to Farm Manager immediately.

Evening — 5:00 PM

  1. Dip boots. Wash hands. Unlock and enter.
  2. Conduct full Evening Observation Walkthrough.
  3. Report any deaths or newly sick birds immediately — do not wait until morning.
  4. Top up all drinkers. Give evening feed. Close vents for the night. Fill in Form A.
  5. Step out. Lock padlock. Hand Form A to Farm Manager for daily review and signature.
SEC 6Feed Guide — Broilers & Turkey▼
⚠ Anti-Sudden Death Rule (Weeks 4–8): Never dump full feed at once. Distribute evenly across all feeders. Ensure constant clean water at all times.
