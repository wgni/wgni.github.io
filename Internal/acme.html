Project timeline — Phase 1 civil works
7 May 2026 — Today
Sand filling ongoing · Gate pillar correction · Factory flooring commencing
Sand filling of Staff Quarters foundation active, 6-inch reinforcement block course in place. Gate pillar chiseling ongoing — filing and plastering to follow. Concrete mixer delivered May 6. Factory building flooring expected to commence today, sequence: gravel, screed, epoxy.
6 May 2026
Concrete mixer delivered · Gate pillar chiseling commenced
Concrete mixer mobilised to site. Gate pillar chiseling commenced. Square pipe (3mm), chisel and mallet procured for correction works.
30 April 2026
Gate casting complete · Perimeter fencing complete · 840 blocks moulded
Gate structure finalised and casting completed. Perimeter fencing completed on all sides — extra block courses added for uniform height. 28 bags cement used producing approximately 840 hollow blocks. Pillar iron (8mm, 10mm rods, wire mesh) received and staged. 200 bags Dangote cement received, stored in factory building.
21–29 April 2026
Staff quarters foundation block work complete · Transformer house complete
Foundation digging commenced April 23. 9-inch hollow blocks laid 3–5 courses below ground, 1 course above. Block work completed April 29. Transformer house floored, plastered, and mesh enclosure with barbed wire completed. Multiple sand trips received on site.
22–23 April 2026
Floor breaking 40% complete · Foundation digging commenced · Fencing completed
Factory building floor breaking ongoing by manual labour, 40% complete by April 23. Foundation digging started for Admin Quarters on April 22, redirected to Staff Quarters on April 23 as priority. Perimeter fencing completed across all boundaries — small section left deliberately for sand backfilling around Admin Quarters.
20 April 2026
Back fencing complete · Gate installation commenced · Left side at 80%
Back perimeter fencing fully completed. Left side fencing at 80% completion. Gate installation commenced April 20. Block moulding continued: 770 units of 6-inch blocks produced (22 bags). Transformer physically installed but not yet connected to grid.
18 April 2026
Factory floor deficiency identified · Decision to refloor · 895 blocks moulded
Epoxy contractor inspected factory floor and identified structural deficiency: only sand was used as sub-base with no gravel layer, causing widespread cracking. Decision taken to break and refloor the entire factory space before any epoxy application proceeds. 895 units of 9-inch hollow blocks moulded on site.
17 April 2026
Solar cameras installed · Transformer installation commenced · Left side block laying
Solar security camera poles set and cameras installed on site. Transformer installation commenced and was expected to be completed April 17. Left side perimeter block laying commenced following completion of foundation blinding. Three-person block laying team deployed across left side and back perimeter.
16 April 2026
Materials received · 1,050 blocks moulded · Left side blinding complete · Gate received
30 tonnes stone sand, 30 tonnes gravel, and 30 tonnes granite/stone dust received from Ibadan. Gate received on site, installation planned for April 20. Left side foundation blinding completed (100%). 1,050 units of 6-inch hollow blocks moulded (30 bags, 35 per bag). Residential Quarters site marking carried out. Two taps constructed on site. Back perimeter block laying continued by one artisan (negotiation breakdown with expected teams).
15 April 2026
First site report · Epoxy first coat commenced · Block laying active
Epoxy paint application commenced on factory building floor. Back section and right half completed, approximately 50% of total floor area. Perimeter fencing block laying active on back and left sections. 260 bags Elephant ECOPlanet cement confirmed on site across inner store and factory building.
